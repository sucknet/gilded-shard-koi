# The Gilded Shard Koi

An ornate gilded picture frame hangs in a dark museum hall, shattering mid-moment as a colossal molten-gold koi bursts through it, shards of carved gold leaf suspended in the air while the great fish, rendered in liquid gleaming metal, leaps toward the viewer out of the canvas and into the room.
